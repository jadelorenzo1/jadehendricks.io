# SA Startup Awards 2026 finalists: Healthtech and Informal Market

Research date: 18 Nov 2026. Scope: Healthtech (GreenNotes, Obiflex, Radhiant Diagnostic Imaging SA, The Surgical Assistant (PTY) Ltd, Traumsformed) and Informal Market (2Klip, AskMandla, FIRST JOBLY).

General note: the official finalist page (startupclubza.com/awards) loaded without content when fetched, so I could not check the finalist list against it. Process context: entries closed 30 Oct 2026, public voting ran 2-6 Nov 2026, and up to five startups were shortlisted per category. Winners are announced 18 Nov 2026 in Cape Town — [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/entries-open-for-2026-south-african-startup-awards-747882a); [Innovation Village](https://innovation-village.com/entries-open-for-the-2026-south-african-startup-awards-celebrating-emerging-tech-innovation/). Three of the eight companies mention their own 2026 nomination or finalist status: Obiflex, Traumsformed and AskMandla.

## What exactly does each product do, and for whom?

### Takeaway
Seven of the eight names map to a clear South African company with a live product. Healthtech covers four areas: clinical admin software (GreenNotes), access to treatment (Obiflex for GLP-1 weight loss, Radhiant for mobile mammography), surgical staffing (The Surgical Assistant) and mental health (Traumsformed). Informal Market covers two areas: work and employment (AskMandla for domestic-worker HR and payroll, FirstJobly as a job board) and crowdsourced field data (2Klip).

### Cited Findings

**GreenNotes (GreenNotes Medical)**
- Product: a South African clinical EMR (electronic medical record) with an AI scribe that records consultations and writes the notes. It also does letters and referrals, scripts, online booking, digital consent, intake forms and an optional Billing & Claims (medical aid) module. It runs on iOS, Android and web — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za)
- Customers: GPs, specialists, allied-health practices and clinics. The site lists over 40 professions, including physios, psychologists, dietitians and OTs — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za)
- Pricing: R960 per clinician per month for Clinical, plus R480 for Billing & Claims and a per-claim fee of R9 or 1.95%. There is a 14-day free trial — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za)
- Marketing describes it as "POPIA-compliant, AI-powered EMR" built "to help clinicians reclaim their time" — [LinkedIn (David Howe post)](https://za.linkedin.com/in/david-howe-a1797960)
- Company: GreenNotes (Pty) Ltd, at Shamrock Office Park, 97 York Street, George. The site footer reads "© 2024–2026" — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za)
- Traction (self-reported): over 4 million minutes transcribed, over 200,000 consultations, and app ratings of 5.0 (App Store) and 4.9 (Google Play). Partner discounts are offered through ADSA, iZandla and PALPRAC — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za)

**Obiflex**
- Product: a doctor-led GLP-1 weight-management programme delivered over WhatsApp, paid in instalments — [LinkedIn posts (Riaz Motara)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/riaz-motara-7ab47847/)
- Pricing in posts: 3 months for R1,499 over 5 payments, and 6 months for R1,999 over 9 payments — [LinkedIn (Riaz Motara)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/riaz-motara-7ab47847/)
- Website: obiflex.co.za, tagline "your weight, your terms". The page content did not render when fetched — [obiflex.co.za](https://obiflex.co.za)
- People named in LinkedIn posts: Shaun Barns (PhD), Riaz Motara (CEO, Brandmed Group), Moshe Unterslak (calls himself co-founder) and Jeff Miller. Unterslak and Miller are linked to Grovest — [Moshe Unterslak](https://www.linkedin.com/in/moshe-unterslak-b4a502156/); [Jeff Miller](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeff-miller-1782413b/)
- Named a Healthtech finalist, SA Startup Awards 2026 (self-reported on LinkedIn) — [LinkedIn (Riaz Motara)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/riaz-motara-7ab47847/)

**Radhiant Diagnostic Imaging SA**
- Service: mobile mammography (3D), mobile ultrasound and diagnostic reporting, using mobile clinics built on FUSO trucks. It is billed as "South Africa's first fully mobile mammography service" — [press release (MarketersMedia, Jul 2025)](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service); [Focus on Transport](https://focusontransport.co.za/?p=41505)
- Founder and CEO: Dr Anith Chacko, a radiologist (MBBCh Wits, FC Rad Diag SA, PhD Bristol). The idea came in 2022 and the service launched in 2023. The base is East London, Eastern Cape, and it targets towns such as Port Alfred, Makhanda, Fort Beaufort and Butterworth — [press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service); [LinkedIn](https://za.linkedin.com/in/dr-anith-chacko)
- Customers and partners: the press release names medical-aid and corporate partners BankMed, MedShield and Medicross. There is also a related Radhiant Foundation for underserved women — [press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service)
- Traction (company-reported, and the figures conflict): "over 10,000 women across more than 300 locations" in the 2025 release, against "over 250 locations" on another listing — [press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service); [intch.org](https://intch.org/company/radhiant-diagnostic-imaging)
- Award: "Best Mobile Mammography Service in South Africa of 2025", given by BestofBestReview.com. This is a pay-to-feature style review site, not an independent industry award — [press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service)

**The Surgical Assistant (PTY) Ltd (TSA)**
- Product: a digital marketplace that matches private-sector surgeons who need a theatre assistant with doctors who want paid work and surgical experience, often junior doctors after community service. It is described as SA's first digital surgical staffing marketplace — [Daily Maverick, Dec 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-12-17-surgical-assistant-is-just-what-sas-unemployed-doctors-ordered); [Bizcommunity, Mar 2026](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/the-surgical-assistant-new-digital-platform-to-ease-south-african-public-health-specialists-dilemma-288767a)
- Founders: Dr Le Roux Viljoen (Cape Town medical officer) and AD Schoeman. Founded October 2018 — [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-12-17-surgical-assistant-is-just-what-sas-unemployed-doctors-ordered)
- Business model: a 15% finder's commission on each placement. It also arranges cover and liability insurance and helps doctors register with private hospital groups — [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-12-17-surgical-assistant-is-just-what-sas-unemployed-doctors-ordered)
- Traction (Dec 2024): over 2,000 potential assistants, over 100 surgeons and about 200 surgeries a month — [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-12-17-surgical-assistant-is-just-what-sas-unemployed-doctors-ordered)
- Other: a Cape Chamber of Commerce award nomination form from 2026 — [Cape Chamber PDF](https://capechamber.co.za/sites/default/files/2026-04/2%20Nomination%20form%20as%20per_V4%20JL_MT2_0.docx%20%281%29%20surgical%20assistant.pdf). Feature in Lifestyle & Tech, Mar 2026 — [Lifestyle & Tech](https://lifestyleandtech.co.za/business/article/2026-03-16/turning-austerity-into-opportunity)

**Traumsformed**
- Product: structured, self-guided trauma support. An AI guide called "Robyn" asks users, in their own words with no forms, what has been happening and places them in a starting state (Dysregulation, Stabilisation or Integration). A 12-month curriculum follows, with referral to a human practitioner through the Healist directory — [traumsformed.com](https://www.traumsformed.com/)
- Pricing: a once-off R399 for 12 months, with no auto-renewal and no card stored — [traumsformed.com](https://www.traumsformed.com/)
- Customers: individuals affected by trauma. The founder also pitches it to employers as an add-on to employee assistance programmes (EAPs) — [LinkedIn (Robyn Wolff)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robyn-wolff-4ba85154/)
- Founder: Robyn Wolff. Her LinkedIn lists Christiaan Barnard Memorial Hospital, Cape Town, and the product claims 30+ years of trauma-counselling experience — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robyn-wolff-4ba85154/); [traumsformed.com](https://www.traumsformed.com/)
- Nominated in Healthtech, SA Startup Awards 2026 (self-reported on LinkedIn) — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robyn-wolff-4ba85154/)

**2Klip (by Timbuk2)**
- Product: a game built on players' knowledge of real places. In "Pin It", for example, players read a clue and drop a pin on a map. Locals also answer field questions for rewards, such as whether a billboard is up or a product is on a shelf. It grew out of Timbuk2's FOONA field-collection network, whose contributors have been paid to map shops (including informal outlets), check billboards and report flood damage — [TechFinancials, 11 Nov 2026](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/11/11/ai-still-needs-people-on-the-ground-inside-south-africas-2klip/); [timbuk2.ai](https://www.timbuk2.ai/)
- Customers (paying side): no current 2Klip customers are named. The target is AI and data teams, retailers (shelf presence and pricing) and advertisers (campaign verification). Timbuk2's past work includes billboard audience measurement and sales-intelligence pilots — [TechFinancials](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/11/11/ai-still-needs-people-on-the-ground-inside-south-africas-2klip/)
- Founder: Matimba Julian Nkuna (Timbuk2). City: Cape Town. Website: timbuk2.ai/2klip — [TechFinancials](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/11/11/ai-still-needs-people-on-the-ground-inside-south-africas-2klip/)
- Traction: over 50,000 sign-ups in the November analysis, after a marketing push from September. The article stresses these are registrations, not people paid — [TechFinancials](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/11/11/ai-still-needs-people-on-the-ground-inside-south-africas-2klip/)

**AskMandla**
- Product: HR and payroll for private households that employ domestic workers, run entirely over WhatsApp (an AI agent, "Mandla", with humans stepping in when needed). It covers employment contracts, UIF and COIDA registration and admin, payday and payslips, and leave tracking. Workers get leave updates and WhatsApp support. There is also a small-business offering — [askmandla.com/about](https://askmandla.com/about/); [askmandla.com/for-employees](https://askmandla.com/for-employees); [We Are Tech Africa](https://wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/solutions/askmandla-targets-1-6-million-domestic-workers-with-whatsapp-based-employment-platform)
- Pricing: once-off UIF registration for R450. The site's calculator shows a R49 fee, but the period is not stated — [askmandla.com/about](https://askmandla.com/about/)
- Team: Peter Adolphs (co-founder and CEO), Janine Kane-Berman (co-founder and CLO), Ean Barnard (Chief Growth Officer) and Stephan Schoeman (Head of Engineering). Registered as Ask Mandla (Pty) Ltd — [askmandla.com/about](https://askmandla.com/about/)
- Traction: reported R5 million in wages processed in its first year — [Portal ERP, Jun 2026](https://portalerp.com/za/noticia/askmandla-uses-whatsapp-to-formalise-domestic-work-in-south-africa). The site states its SA Startup Awards 2026 nomination — [askmandla.com/about](https://askmandla.com/about/)

**FIRST JOBLY (FirstJobly)**
- Product: a South African job board for government vacancies, learnerships, internships, graduate programmes, apprenticeships and bursaries, updated daily across all 9 provinces. It offers a free CV Maker, a Z83 form filler (the government job-application form), job alerts, application tracking, an Android app with one-tap apply, a WhatsApp channel and a community forum. Employers can post jobs — [firstjobly.co.za](https://firstjobly.co.za)
- Customers: first-time and entry-level job seekers and graduates, plus employers — [firstjobly.co.za](https://firstjobly.co.za)
- No founders, address, founding year or traction figures are shown on the homepage — [firstjobly.co.za](https://firstjobly.co.za)

### Inferences
- GreenNotes was probably founded in about 2024 (inferred from the copyright line) and is based in George, Western Cape.
- Obiflex looks like a venture backed by Grovest-linked people (Grovest is an SA Section 12J/VC-style investment group). That is consistent with an early-stage company, but no funding round is documented.
- 2Klip fits "Informal Market" mainly because its contributors are township and informal-economy residents paid for micro-tasks, and because it collects data on informal retail. It is not a tool for informal traders themselves.
- FirstJobly is focused on formal, entry-level and government jobs. Its fit with "Informal Market" is presumably about youth unemployment, job access and the Z83 tooling.

### Gaps
- Founders, founding year and funding were not found for GreenNotes, FirstJobly and Traumsformed (founder known, year not).
- No funding round was found for any of the eight.
- No founding year was found for Obiflex, AskMandla or 2Klip/Timbuk2.
- Obiflex's website did not render, so its offer is known only from LinkedIn posts.

## What pain point does each address (SA context)?

### Takeaway
Each product targets a concrete SA access or cost gap: clinician admin load, unaffordable GLP-1 drugs, no mammography in small Eastern Cape towns, unemployed junior doctors against surgeons short of assistants, scarce trauma care, AI's lack of on-the-ground data from underserved places, informal (non-compliant) domestic employment, and youth job-search friction.

### Cited Findings
- GreenNotes: fragmented practice admin (notes, letters, billing, claim rejections, chasing patients for forms). The aim is to let clinicians "reclaim their time", with POPIA compliance for health data — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za); [LinkedIn](https://za.linkedin.com/in/david-howe-a1797960)
- Obiflex: cost barrier to GLP-1 weight-loss drugs. Posts cite R3,000-R6,000 a month for registered GLP-1 treatments, with medical schemes covering about 50%. Its answer is lower-cost instalment pricing and doctor oversight over WhatsApp — [LinkedIn (Riaz Motara)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/riaz-motara-7ab47847/)
- Radhiant: mammography is not readily available in smaller towns (Port Alfred, Makhanda, Fort Beaufort, Butterworth), so women must travel. The mobile units bring 3D screening to them — [press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service)
- The Surgical Assistant: young doctors struggle to get specialist training posts and paid work, especially after community service (registrations spike each December), while private surgeons need reliable assistants. It "improves how existing skills are deployed" amid public-sector austerity — [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-12-17-surgical-assistant-is-just-what-sas-unemployed-doctors-ordered); [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/the-surgical-assistant-new-digital-platform-to-ease-south-african-public-health-specialists-dilemma-288767a)
- Traumsformed: trauma shows up in relationships, parenting, habits and feeling unsafe, and people often don't see it as trauma. It offers affordable (R399), private, 24/7 structured support with routing to human care — [traumsformed.com](https://www.traumsformed.com/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robyn-wolff-4ba85154/). SA context: the country has very high rates of exposure to violent trauma — [CSVR](https://help.csvr.org.za/?p=675)
- 2Klip: "An AI system can analyse a street photograph. Someone still has to go there and take it." AI and data buyers lack fresh ground-truth on places the internet doesn't cover, and locals gain small earning opportunities — [TechFinancials](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/11/11/ai-still-needs-people-on-the-ground-inside-south-africas-2klip/)
- AskMandla: about 1.6 million SA domestic workers, many outside formal employment and social protection (UIF, COIDA, contracts, payslips). Household employers find compliance confusing. Head of growth Ean Barnard says existing fintech "stops at the employer's door" — [We Are Tech Africa](https://wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/solutions/askmandla-targets-1-6-million-domestic-workers-with-whatsapp-based-employment-platform); [GPA](https://gpa.net/blogs/africa/south-africa-whatsapp-employment-platform-targets-1-6m-domestic-workers)
- FirstJobly: entry-level and government opportunities are scattered across sources, and application paperwork is a hurdle (free CV maker, Z83 filler). It delivers over WhatsApp and Android for mobile-first job seekers — [firstjobly.co.za](https://firstjobly.co.za)

### Inferences
- FirstJobly's problem framing (youth unemployment, access to government posts) comes from its feature set, not from any stated mission. Its "About us" content was not visible.

### Gaps
- No independent outcome data (clinical or impact) for any company. Traumsformed's founder says the evidence is early.

## Is each definitely the right company (SA, early-stage, under 5 years, pre-Series A)?

### Takeaway
Most matches are high confidence. The Surgical Assistant is clearly the right company but appears to have been founded in 2018, which is over the 5-year criterion. FirstJobly is a probable match with almost no company information. Obiflex is a solid match based on its own finalist post.

### Cited Findings
- GreenNotes: SA company (George), with a 2024-2026 copyright line — [greennotes.co.za](https://greennotes.co.za). Confidence: high (healthtech, SA, young). Founders unknown.
- Obiflex: a LinkedIn post says it was named a Healthtech finalist, SA Startup Awards 2026 — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/riaz-motara-7ab47847/). Confidence: high. A name-only search mostly returns Omniflex (Pty) Ltd, an unrelated Durban automation firm founded in 1965; do not confuse the two — [Plant Automation](https://www.plantautomation-technology.com/suppliers/omniflex-pty-ltd)
- Radhiant Diagnostic Imaging SA Inc.: SA (East London), launched 2023 — [press release](https://business.minstercommunitypost.com/minstercommunitypost/article/marketersmedia-2025-7-17-radhiant-diagnostic-imaging-sa-inc-introduces-south-africas-first-fully-mobile-mammography-service). Confidence: high. It is not the US "Radiant Diagnostic Imaging" practices (Florida and elsewhere) — [npichecker](https://npichecker.com/npi/1326397423/radiant-diagnostic-imaging-inc). It is a capital-heavy service business (trucks and imaging equipment) rather than a software startup, and it is linked to a wider "Radhiant Group" — [intch.org](https://intch.org/company/radhiant-diagnostic-imaging)
- The Surgical Assistant: right company (Cape Town, SA), confidence high. Daily Maverick says it was founded in October 2018, making it about 8 years old — [Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-12-17-surgical-assistant-is-just-what-sas-unemployed-doctors-ordered). This conflicts with the awards' "under 5 years" framing, unless the (Pty) Ltd was registered later than the platform began. That is unverified.
- Traumsformed: unique name, its own site, and the founder posts about its Healthtech nomination — [traumsformed.com](https://www.traumsformed.com/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robyn-wolff-4ba85154/). Confidence: high (SA from R pricing and the Cape Town founder).
- 2Klip: TechFinancials profiles "South Africa's 2Klip" (Cape Town, Timbuk2) a week before the awards — [TechFinancials](https://techfinancials.co.za/2026/11/11/ai-still-needs-people-on-the-ground-inside-south-africas-2klip/). Confidence: medium-high. No awards mention was found, and its category fit is indirect.
- AskMandla: the site itself mentions its SA Startup Awards 2026 nomination — [askmandla.com/about](https://askmandla.com/about/). Confidence: very high.
- FIRST JOBLY: firstjobly.co.za is the only SA match found — [firstjobly.co.za](https://firstjobly.co.za). Confidence: medium. The name and spelling match and it is SA-focused, but no awards mention, founders or company details were found.

### Inferences
- None of the eight shows evidence of a Series A. Funding information is absent for all of them, so "pre-Series A" is likely but unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- I could not access the official finalist list on startupclubza.com to cross-check names and descriptions.
- Company registration (CIPC) dates were not checked, so founding years are based on press, the site footer or the founder's own story.
