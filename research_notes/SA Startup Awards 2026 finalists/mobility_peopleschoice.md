# SA Startup Awards 2026 – Mobility & Logistics finalists + People's Choice-only finalists (and winners check)

Research date: 18 Nov 2026 (the day of the ceremony). Startups covered: Bro Cabs (Pty) Ltd, CitiPods, LaaiMy.co.za, RØHM Media (Mobility & Logistics); Guideian, Zande Technologies (People's Choice only).

## What does each startup do, for whom, and what problem does it solve?

### Takeaway
All six were found. **Bro Cabs** (a subscription e-hailing app in Johannesburg), **CitiPods** (rest hubs for delivery couriers in Cape Town), **RØHM Media** (LED screens on top of rideshare cars, Gauteng) and **Guideian** (a career-guidance app for young South Africans) are well identified. **LaaiMy** is a small bakkie-delivery service in George. **Zande Technologies** is very likely a CIPC-registered company from 2025 that makes the Ziyawa events platform and an AI website builder. It is not the older spaza-shop fintech "Zande Africa", which shares the name.

### Cited Findings

**Bro Cabs (Pty) Ltd – Mobility & Logistics (confidence: high)**
- What it is: a ride-hailing (e-hailing) platform where drivers pay a flat subscription instead of a commission on each trip, so they keep 100% of fares. Riders see the price before booking, can track the trip live, and can pay by cash, card or wallet. Website: https://www.brocabs.co.za — [Bro Cabs website](https://www.brocabs.co.za/)
- Driver pricing on the website: R150/week or R600/month. Press also reports a R30/day plan — [Bro Cabs website](https://www.brocabs.co.za/); [TimesLIVE, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/)
- Other features: Bro SCLR, a student scheme with discounted fares after verification; routes to vehicle rental or finance for drivers from R3,500/month; an SOS button linked to SaferCity. The press adds no peak-hour surge pricing and bookings that don't use mobile data — [Bro Cabs website](https://www.brocabs.co.za/); [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/)
- Per TimesLIVE, the subscription fees pay for roadside assistance, emergency medical support, towing, security partnerships, marketing and pay for "dead mileage" (driving without a passenger) — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/)
- Founders: Persy Qamata (CEO), Msizi Mtolo (COO), Luther Mfetane (CSO), Cebile Magudulela (CMO) — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/); [Briefly](https://briefly.co.za/people/247994-4-brother-give-uber-bolt-a-run-money/)
- City: Johannesburg. The website says it serves "118 locations across Johannesburg" and gives its HQ as Morningside, Sandton — [Bro Cabs website](https://www.brocabs.co.za/)
- Founded: launched February 2025 ("launched in February last year" in a July 2026 article) — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/)
- Traction (sources disagree):
  - TimesLIVE: more than 4,900 drivers registered in under a year — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/)
  - TechTrends KE: more than 1,000 drivers by July 2026 — [TechTrends KE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/12/south-africa-ride-hailing-competition/)
  - Techloy: 30,000 drivers, an outlier — [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/brief/south-african-app-bro-cabs-lets-drivers-keep-all-fares-11rk1r2/)
- Funding: no outside funding reported. The founders call funding their biggest challenge and say they grew "organically". One report says the start-up money came from the founders' property-maintenance business — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/); [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/brief/south-african-app-bro-cabs-lets-drivers-keep-all-fares-11rk1r2/)
- Recognition: the website says it was chosen for the 2026 Google for Startups Accelerator: South Africa, one of 15 startups picked from 1,057 applications — [Bro Cabs website](https://www.brocabs.co.za/)
- Problem it solves: Uber and Bolt take a commission on every trip, which squeezes driver income. Riders deal with surge pricing and the cost of mobile data. Press coverage also frames driver safety as an issue — [TechTrends KE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/12/south-africa-ride-hailing-competition/); [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/sci-tech/2026-07-16-how-bro-cabs-beat-the-odds-to-break-into-e-hailing/)

**CitiPods – Mobility & Logistics (confidence: high)**
- What it is: CitiPods designs, builds and runs modular timber rest hubs for delivery drivers. Each pod has benches, covered seating, lighting, lockable access, weather protection, greenery and phone charging. Sites can add a serviced toilet, WiFi, solar power, battery swapping or motorbike parking. Website: https://citipods.co.za — [CitiPods website](https://citipods.co.za/)
- Business model: host sites provide the space, sponsors pay for whole pods or parts of them, and advertisers can buy panels on the pods — [CitiPods website](https://citipods.co.za/)
- Problem it solves: delivery couriers have nowhere to wait, rest, charge a phone or use a toilet between orders. Sites end up with messy waiting areas and drivers blocking pedestrian paths — [CitiPods website](https://citipods.co.za/)
- Co-founder & CEO: Jenny Dove — [Bizcommunity, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/uber-eats-and-citipods-pilot-courier-hub-to-improve-urban-space-in-cape-town-967724a)
- City: Cape Town — [CitiPods website](https://citipods.co.za/)
- Traction: first pilot launched with Uber Eats at Constantia Village, Cape Town (announced 20 Aug 2026). A second pod is built and waiting to be installed at the V&A Waterfront. No usage figures or funding published — [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/uber-eats-and-citipods-pilot-courier-hub-to-improve-urban-space-in-cape-town-967724a); [CitiPods website](https://citipods.co.za/)
- Name clash: an older Bizcommunity piece uses "CitiPods" for an outdoor-advertising format tied to Clear Channel's Citilite range. That is unrelated older usage — [Bizcommunity (older)](https://bizcommunity.com/Article/196/70/15040.html)

**LaaiMy.co.za – Mobility & Logistics (confidence: medium-high that this is the right entity; the only one with that domain)**
- What it is: a local delivery and transport service in George and the Garden Route ("laai" is Afrikaans for "load"). It handles parcels, same-day delivery for bookings made before 2pm, and furniture and large items. Pricing is by distance. It uses a 1-tonne Hyundai H100 bakkie with optional helpers for loading, and offers live GPS tracking and WhatsApp notifications. Rubble removal is excluded — [LaaiMy website](https://laaimy.co.za)
- Customers: anyone in George needing parcels, furniture or appliances moved, including people in Knysna, Mossel Bay, Plettenberg Bay, Wilderness and Oudtshoorn. The site names no specific type of customer — [LaaiMy website](https://laaimy.co.za)
- Problem it solves (inferred from the website): there are few on-demand, trackable small-load delivery options in a regional town outside the big metros — [LaaiMy website](https://laaimy.co.za)
- Owner: the footer says "a YOBO Studio company". Founders and founding year are not stated (© 2026 only) — [LaaiMy website](https://laaimy.co.za)
- No press coverage found — [WebSearch returned no relevant results; see Gaps]

**RØHM Media (ROHM Media) – Mobility & Logistics (confidence: high)**
- What it is: mobile out-of-home advertising (ads seen outside the home), with three channels:
  - LED screens on the roofs of rideshare cars, about 200 active screens
  - in-car WiFi portals that show passengers branded content and surveys
  - programmatic or "dynamic" ads that change with the weather, time, location or temperature
  - It also offers add-ons such as product sampling and branded headrest covers. Website: https://rohmmedia.co.za (rohmmedia.com also resolves) — [RØHM Media website](https://rohmmedia.co.za/)
- Customers: brands and marketers. Campaigns shown on the site include Valor, Bridgement, YogiPro and Medihelp — [RØHM Media website](https://rohmmedia.co.za/)
- Problem it solves: static billboards can't follow people as they move around. Moving screens reach commuters across city routes, and they also give rideshare drivers extra income. That last point is inferred from the site describing drivers working shifts — [RØHM Media website](https://rohmmedia.co.za/)
- Region: Johannesburg and Pretoria (Gauteng) — [RØHM Media website](https://rohmmedia.co.za/)
- Traction claims on its own site:
  - 200 active screens
  - "50M+" impressions a month
  - 250K views per vehicle per month in the Joburg metro
  - cites a Nielsen OOH study finding 84% of people noticed cartop ads in the past month
  - [RØHM Media website](https://rohmmedia.co.za/)
- Founders and founding year: not found. A LinkedIn profile (Darian Beck, linked to YogiPro, one of the brands on the site) shows connections at ROHM Media, but that is not evidence of who founded it — [LinkedIn](https://za.linkedin.com/in/darian-beck-012277172)

**Guideian – People's Choice (confidence: high)**
- What it is: a career-guidance app for iOS and Android. It has three parts:
  - Career Recommender: matches users to qualifications they can get into, based on their marks and interests
  - Insights Dashboard: shows which skills South Africa's job market wants
  - CV Toolkit: builds a CV and checks it against the applicant tracking systems (ATS) employers use to filter CVs
  - Website: https://www.guideian.co.za (guideian.com also resolves) — [Guideian website](https://www.guideian.co.za/)
- Customers: young people aged 16–30, plus B2B clients such as tutoring companies, schools, NGOs, funders and recruiters — [Guideian website](https://www.guideian.co.za/)
- Problem it solves (implied on the site): young South Africans struggle to pick a qualification they can actually get into, to see where the jobs are, and to present themselves to employers. This fits SA's youth unemployment problem — [Guideian website](https://www.guideian.co.za/)
- Awards listed on its site:
  - EDHE Absa Entrepreneurship InnoVarsity 2026: Western Cape regional winner (Social Impact) and national finalist
  - Nedbank YouthX Awards 2026: Top 50
  - SA Startup Awards 2026: People's Choice finalist
  - MTN Enactus Digital Innovation Challenge 2025: Top 10 finalist
  - It shows logos of UCT, UWC, MTN, Enactus and the Hult Prize Foundation
  - [Guideian website](https://www.guideian.co.za/)
- Founders, city and founding year: not stated. The UCT/UWC logos and the Western Cape regional win point to a Cape Town, student-founded venture, but this is unconfirmed — [Guideian website](https://www.guideian.co.za/)

**Zande Technologies – People's Choice (confidence: medium)**
- Best candidate: Zande Technologies (Pty) Ltd. It is registered with CIPC (South Africa's companies register) under K2025834311 and with the Information Regulator (2025-066656), so it was registered in 2025. It builds and runs **Ziyawa**, described as an "Event Operating System – built in South Africa, for South Africa" — [Ziyawa About page](https://www.ziyawa.com/about)
- What Ziyawa does:
  - one platform for the whole event process: ticketing with QR-code tickets, booking artists and crew, a crew/vendor marketplace, QR check-in, messaging, reviews and in-app wallets
  - payments run through Paystack and are held in escrow until after the event
  - ticket revenue goes to the organiser 48 hours after the event, and artists and crew are paid after the event
  - [Ziyawa About page](https://www.ziyawa.com/about)
- Problem it solves:
  - organisers juggle separate tools to sell tickets, book artists and hire vendors
  - artists struggle to get discovered
  - service providers chase unpaid invoices
  - "groovists" (event-goers) can't tell whether an event is legitimate
  - Zande frames this around SA "groove culture"
  - [Ziyawa About page](https://www.ziyawa.com/about)
- Partner: Rath Group (Pty) Ltd — [Ziyawa About page](https://www.ziyawa.com/about)
- Zande's own site (zande.io) is titled "Zande | AI Website and Web App Builder", a no-code AI website builder. A search snippet of its terms page listed a Johannesburg address and director Zwonaka Mabege. I could not fetch the page to confirm this because it renders with JavaScript — [zande.io](https://www.zande.io/); [zande.io terms (search snippet only)](https://www.zande.io/terms)
- Not the same company (name clashes):
  - Zande Africa, formerly Invoiceworx, a spaza-shop credit and logistics fintech founded around 2015–17 by Siya Ntutela and Mdu Thabethe, based in Mpumalanga. It is too old to qualify (over 5 years) and PitchBook lists it as out of business — [Ventureburn 2019](https://ventureburn.com/2019/03/fintech-zande-spaza-shops/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/183593-98)
  - zandetechnologies.com is "Z & E Technologies Incorporated", which makes casual puzzle games with no SA link — [zandetechnologies.com](https://zandetechnologies.com/)
  - zande.net is "Zande Fintech Solutions (Pty) Ltd", another SA "technology studio" with a different registration number — [zande.net](https://zande.net/)

### Inferences
- **Bro Cabs** fits the brief: South African, founded 2025, bootstrapped, so pre-Series A. The most credible driver count is roughly 1,000–4,900. The 30,000 figure looks like an error.
- **CitiPods** fits as early-stage. Its first pilot only launched in Aug 2026. Founding year not confirmed, but probably 2025–26.
- **LaaiMy** looks like a very small local service business, possibly a side venture of a studio called "YOBO Studio". It is the least tech-heavy and least documented finalist in the group.
- **RØHM Media** fits Mobility & Logistics as an ad-tech business that runs on vehicles, not as a transport provider.
- **Zande Technologies**: the K2025 registration fits the under-5-years rule, so the Ziyawa/zande.io company is the best match, at medium confidence. The link between zande.io and zande.net is unclear.

### Gaps
- No founders or founding year found for CitiPods (beyond CEO Jenny Dove), LaaiMy, RØHM Media or Guideian.
- The director name for Zande Technologies comes only from a search-engine summary and was not verified directly.
- No funding information found for any of the six. Bro Cabs is explicitly bootstrapped.
- No details found on "YOBO Studio", LaaiMy's parent company.
- The startupclubza.com/awards page loads its content with JavaScript, and neither fetch method could read the finalist write-ups. So none of the identifications could be cross-checked against the organiser's own descriptions. Exception: Guideian's site itself names the SA Startup Awards People's Choice nomination.
