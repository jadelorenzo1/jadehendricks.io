# SA Startup Awards 2026: five hard-to-identify finalists

Research date: 18 Nov 2026. The official finalist list at startupclubza.com/awards is a JavaScript single-page app with no readable server-rendered content (fetch returned only the title), so each match below rests on the company's own site or posts, LinkedIn and press. All five were identified. Logos were not visually compared except where noted.

## 1. Brand Scan (AI & Big Data)

### Takeaway
**Brand Scan (brandscan.co.za), from Cape Town, founded by Odette van Wyk. Confidence: high on the identity, medium-high on the finalist link.** It is a generative engine optimisation (GEO) company. It audits, tracks and improves how often a brand is recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT. It works mainly with enterprise FMCG and services brands. It is not counterfeit detection, despite what the logo suggests.

### Cited Findings
- The site's tagline is "When customers ask AI what to buy, Brand Scan makes sure your brand is the answer." It audits, tracks and optimises brand visibility across AI platforms. It gives enterprise leaders intelligence on "AI market share, competitor preference" and the actions needed to win conversational search. — [brandscan.co.za](https://brandscan.co.za/)
- Method: multi-model scans, an authority benchmark tracked month to month, and a "Prioritised Remediation Roadmap". It also trains a custom Small Language Model / AI agent for each client from that client's scan data. Ready-made prompt libraries cover FMCG and short-term insurance. — [brandscan.co.za](https://brandscan.co.za/)
- Traction claim: "We have worked with major FMCG brands on their GEO strategies. One brand's Brand Scan score rose by 37 points after remediation." — [brandscan.co.za](https://brandscan.co.za/)
- The site footer shows "Brand Scan 2026 · Registration Number 2025/431869/07". The 2025 prefix and the 07 suffix mean a South African (Pty) company registered with CIPC in 2025. Contact: odette@brandscan.co.za. LinkedIn: linkedin.com/company/brand-scan-ai. — [brandscan.co.za](https://brandscan.co.za/)
- Odette van Wyk is listed as "Founder: Brand Scan at Brand Scan AI", Cape Town. — [RocketReach (aggregator)](https://rocketreach.co/odette-van-wyk-email_7365489); [LinkedIn: Odette V. - Brand Scan AI](https://www.linkedin.com/in/odettevanwyk/)
- A Kimberly-Clark digital marketing manager's LinkedIn post (seen only as a search snippet) mentions a GEO pilot with Brand Scan AI. — [LinkedIn search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simone-dolley-579888172/)

### Inferences
- The "scanning lens" logo fits "scanning" AI engines for brand mentions. The AI & Big Data category also fits. Founded in 2025, so well under 5 years old. Probably bootstrapped or pre-seed.
- Do not confuse it with aibrandscan.com, which turned up in search as an agency audit tool. I did not confirm whether it is connected to this company.

### Gaps
- I found no finalist post from Brand Scan itself, so the finalist link rests on the unique name, the SA registration and the category fit.
- Co-founders and funding are unknown. The Kimberly-Clark pilot comes from a snippet only and was not verified.

## 2. KRITIQ (AI & Big Data)

### Takeaway
**KRITIQ (kritiq.app, plus a services arm, KRITIQ Studio), from Johannesburg, founded by Pieter Geyser. Confidence: high.** Its own site lists it as a finalist in "AI & Big Data Startup Awards 2026". It is an AI marketing-intelligence platform. It puts ads, web traffic, content, SEO and AI-search visibility into one dashboard, tells marketers what to fix first, and drafts the fix.

### Cited Findings
- Tagline: "All your marketing. One clear answer." It joins ads, website traffic, content, SEO and AI search in one dashboard, then recommends and drafts fixes. It is built for brand marketers, agencies, performance/media teams and content/SEO teams. — [kritiq.app](https://kritiq.app/)
- Pricing is in ZAR, excluding VAT: Validator R2,499/month, Accelerator R4,499/month, Alpha R7,499/month, and Enterprise on request. Every plan has a 14-day free trial. The footer reads "(Pty) Ltd". — [kritiq.app](https://kritiq.app/)
- KRITIQ Studio is the solutions arm. Services: e-commerce, web development, measurement, SEO/AEO, paid media, influencer/UGC and AI automation. It is headquartered in Johannesburg and names Pieter Geyser as founder. The page lists brands Geyser has worked with (Unilever, PepsiCo, Coca-Cola, Google, TikTok and others) but does not call them KRITIQ clients. The page shows "Finalist in the AI & Big Data Startup Awards 2026". Contact: hello@kritiq.app. Product modules: KRITIQ Creative, KRITIQ Data, KRITIQ BMI. — [kritiqstudio.com](https://kritiqstudio.com/)

### Inferences
- The "Q in viewfinder brackets" logo probably stands for "seeing" or measuring marketing. The name is a play on "critique", meaning a diagnosis of what is wrong with your marketing.

### Gaps
- Founding year, co-founders, user numbers and funding were not found.
- Unrelated namesakes to avoid: kritik.ai, an art-critique iOS app, and a GitHub KRITIQ code-review project.

## 3. Kwanda AI (AI & Big Data + People's Choice)

### Takeaway
**Kwanda AI (kwanda.ai), Stellenbosch/Cape Town, co-founded by Chris Immelman and Kieran Donnelly. Confidence: high.** It is an "AI enablement partner". It designs, deploys and maintains custom AI agents and automations for businesses, built on a company-specific AI "brain" connected to the firm's systems. It is the first formal spin-out of Adii Pienaar's AI venture studio Ubundi. Its new product, Ayda, was named in its award nomination. It is NOT the London diaspora fund "Kwanda".

### Cited Findings
- Kwanda AI is a Cape Town-based AI enablement partner that "designs, deploys and maintains AI agents and automations for businesses". It builds the infrastructure that makes agents useful and offers custom AI roadmaps. Founders: Chris Immelman and Kieran Donnelly. Clients span VC, events, procurement, engineering and legal; none are named. Undated, but internal cues suggest May 2026. — [The Open Letter: Kwanda AI Enablement](https://theopenletter.io/p/kwanda-ai-enablement)
- Website tagline: "AI strategists and engineers in your business." — [kwanda.ai](https://kwanda.ai/)
- From search-result summaries: Kwanda is the first formal spin-out of Ubundi, the AI venture studio founded by Adii Pienaar. Donnelly holds a UCT Data Science MSc and previously worked at ExploreAI and Sand Technologies. The listed address is 21 Mark Street, Stellenbosch. — [The Open Letter: SA founders May](https://theopenletter.io/p/sa-founders-may); [Stellenbosch Network: Kwanda](https://www.stellenboschnetwork.co.za/members/kwanda/); [LinkedIn: Kieran Donnelly - Kwanda AI](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kbdonnelly/)
- A company LinkedIn post (search snippet) says Kwanda, "along with our brand new product, Ayda", was nominated for Startup Club ZA's 2026 South African Startup Awards. Another post says Kwanda AI was shortlisted for (Innovation City/The Open Letter's) "Startup of the Year". — [LinkedIn: Kieran Donnelly](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kbdonnelly/)

### Inferences
- An existing Cape Town startup-community audience (Open Letter, Ubundi, Startup of the Year) would explain its strong People's Choice vote. Founding is likely 2025 or 2026, which makes it very young.
- This is B2B business-automation AI, not township, agri or youth AI as the original brief guessed.

### Gaps
- What Ayda does, the founding year and funding were not found. The details come mostly from self-published posts.

## 4. Tavas (Deeptech)

### Takeaway
**Tavas Technologies (tavas.tech), Cape Town, founded in 2024 by Dirk Pauw (ex-Tesla microgrid/energy-storage engineer). Confidence: high.** Pauw's LinkedIn reportedly promotes Tavas as a Deeptech finalist at the SA Startup Awards 2026. It makes AI-powered autonomous energy management: an NVIDIA-powered edge device plus a cloud platform. The system configures itself on solar, battery and microgrid sites, optimises cost in real time, keeps critical loads running through grid outages, and predicts maintenance.

### Cited Findings
- Products: an Edge AI device (NVIDIA-powered), a SaaS cloud platform, a protocol gateway for mixed assets, and zero-trust security. Three AI pillars: AI Operator, AI Optimizer (forecasting/cost) and AI Support (monitoring/predictive maintenance). It offers automatic islanding, load balancing and failover. It is aimed at energy security, grid resilience and access in remote areas and emerging markets. — [tavas.tech](https://www.tavas.tech/)
- Claimed results (no baselines given): 98% faster deployment, 68% cost reduction, 18%+ efficiency gains. — [tavas.tech](https://www.tavas.tech/)
- Founded in 2024 by CEO Dirk Pauw. He holds a BEng in electrical and electronic engineering from Stellenbosch (2005) and was at Tesla from 2016 to 2023: project engineer for Africa, microgrid applications engineer for EMEA, then product manager. Article dated 24 Sep 2026. — [We Are Tech Africa (FR)](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/dirk-pauw-deploie-une-ia-qui-optimise-lenergie-en-temps-reel)
- Pauw is listed as Founder & CEO of Tavas Technologies Inc, Cape Town. — [RocketReach (aggregator)](https://rocketreach.co/dirk-pauw-email_112949773)
- His LinkedIn (seen as a search summary) promotes a Deeptech finalist spot at the SA Startup Awards 2026 and selection for the Electrify Nevada Fall 2026 cohort. — [LinkedIn: Dirk Pauw - Tavas Technologies Inc](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dirk-pauw/)

### Inferences
- The "Inc" suffix and the Nevada cohort suggest a US (likely Delaware) parent with operations in South Africa. That is common for SA startups raising abroad, but it is not confirmed.
- Load-shedding and grid instability in SA make the problem relevant locally.

### Gaps
- Customers, pilots and funding were not found. The finalist and Electrify Nevada claims come from a search summary of LinkedIn and were not read directly. Unrelated namesakes: Tavus (a US AI video company) and Indian "Tavas Technologies" firms.

## 5. Fluent (Edtech)

### Takeaway
**Fluent AI (Pty) Ltd (getfluent.co.za), Cape Town, co-founded by JT Cronje in April 2026. Confidence: medium-high.** A LinkedIn post (search snippet) says Fluent was shortlisted for the Startup Club ZA SA Startup Awards. The site's palette (orange #ff5900, dark grey #4d4f57) matches the orange/grey logo. It is workplace AI-literacy training. A free "AI audit" scores teams, followed by a six-session "AI Zero to One" programme on the team's own workflows, executive sessions and a personalised learning platform. That places it in edtech as corporate learning, not schools.

### Cited Findings
- "AI enablement, training and consulting for professional teams. We audit where your team actually stands, then build the fluency to close the gap." Legal name: Fluent AI (Pty) Ltd. Country: ZA. Contact: sales@getfluent.co.za. Socials: linkedin.com/company/fluent-ai-training, instagram.com/fluentai_. — [getfluent.co.za (schema.org data)](https://getfluent.co.za/)
- Offering: a free AI audit scoring delegation, description, discernment and diligence; executive sessions; six-session training; a personalised learning platform (live); AI governance (coming soon). It claims "27+ teams already enabled". In an Old School case study, AI champions rose from 14 to 27 and the share of staff comfortable with AI went from 46% to 54%. Testimonials from ZAQ Actuaries, Du Roi Nursery, PHA and NRB Risk Solutions. — [getfluent.co.za](https://getfluent.co.za/)
- JT Cronje is co-founder of Fluent from April 2026 and is based in Cape Town. Fluent grew out of a Tomorrow Today webinar; Tomorrow Today is the AI newsletter he co-writes with Anton Prinsloo. It has trained 27+ companies in its first months across agriculture, financial services, retail, logistics and mining. — [jtcronje.co.za](https://www.jtcronje.co.za/)
- A LinkedIn post (search snippet): "Fluent has been shortlisted for the Startup Club ZA South African Startup Awards, and we're a finalist in the OPUS Investec ESEY's". — search result summary, source post not opened (related: [jtcronje.co.za](https://www.jtcronje.co.za/))

### Inferences
- It is about 7 months old and bootstrapped, with revenue from training. Anton Prinsloo may be a co-founder or trainer; a testimonial names "JT and Anton" as trainers, but this is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- The full co-founder list and funding are unknown. The finalist claim rests on one LinkedIn snippet plus the matching name and colours. A different SA reading or literacy app called "Fluent" was not found anywhere.

## Method / what was tried
- Searches by name plus South Africa, plus the award, and plus LinkedIn. Fetched company sites. Probed .co.za, .ai and .io domain variants with curl: brandscan.co.za and getfluent.co.za resolved. The Startup Club ZA awards page is a client-rendered app and yielded no finalist text. I did not try to read its backend data store.
