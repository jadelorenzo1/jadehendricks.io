## What exactly does each product do, and for whom? What problem does it solve?

### Takeaway
Five of the nine finalists could be identified. Community Wolf runs WhatsApp-based crime reporting. Subverted does offensive cybersecurity, mainly penetration testing. C Star turns CO2 into liquid fuels. Nanosene makes polymers for membrane-protein and drug-target research. SafeGuard Technologies builds a panic button, app and drone safety system. Brand Scan, KRITIQ, Kwanda AI and Tavas returned no reliable South African match.

### Cited Findings

**Community Wolf (AI & Big Data)**
- What it does: a WhatsApp-based public-safety platform for reporting crime and suspicious activity and gathering community intelligence. It also aims to be a connecting layer and data-insights layer for the wider public-safety industry. — [ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/whatsapp-based-public-safety-platform-gets-r83m/KA3Ww7dznBYqrydZ)
- Residents report through WhatsApp. They need no app download and no special hardware. — [Tech In Africa](https://www.techinafrica.com/?p=40137)
- Problem: crime reporting and emergency response in South Africa are slow and fragmented. The Namola deal is framed as "addressing fragmented response". — [Press Portal](https://pressportal.co.za/stream/story/d1d85b3929f105bf-20251217.html)
- Co-founders: Nick Mills and Michael Houghton. Website: communitywolf.com. — [ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/whatsapp-based-public-safety-platform-gets-r83m/KA3Ww7dznBYqrydZ)
- Funding: £340,000 (about R8.3m), led by UK VC Fuel Ventures, reported May 2025. — [ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/whatsapp-based-public-safety-platform-gets-r83m/KA3Ww7dznBYqrydZ)
- Earlier funding: investment from The Baobab Network, reported January 2025 (reported elsewhere as $100k). — [WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2025/01/22/baobab-network-invests-communitywolf/); [techandbiz](https://techandbiz.com.ng/2025/04/09/community-wolf-transforms-whatsapp-into-an-ai-driven-crime-prevention-platform-for-all-south-africans)
- Acquisition: in December 2025 it bought Namola, an emergency-response app that connects users to police, medical, fire and private security responders. Namola keeps running as a standalone product. — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/?p=175575); [TechMoran](https://techmoran.com/?p=223957); [StartupMag](https://startupmag.co.za/?p=95666); [WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2025/12/08/community-wolf-acquires-namola-ai-safety-network/)
- Also has a consumer "Wolf Security" iOS app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/wolf-security/id6748885444)

**Subverted (AI & Big Data)**
- Describes itself as building "proactive security for the human-AI era". It has three lines of business:
  - expert-led penetration testing (application, network, cloud and consulting)
  - "Proactive", a unified offensive-security platform, in private beta with selected enterprise customers
  - Subverted Academy, gamified cyber-skills training
  — [ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/south-african-start-up-subverted-challenges-norms-of-proactive-security-at-itweb-security-summit-2026/rxP3jqBEbWgMA2ye)
- Problem: offensive-security programmes rely on disconnected tools and providers. That causes noise, duplicated effort and gaps where risk goes unnoticed. Proactive aims to give one continuous view of the attack surface and one workflow for acting on it. — [ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/south-african-start-up-subverted-challenges-norms-of-proactive-security-at-itweb-security-summit-2026/rxP3jqBEbWgMA2ye)
- Customers: enterprises in South Africa and beyond. — same source
- Founders: Ulrich Swart (CEO) and Jason Spencer (CTO), long-time penetration testers with close to a decade in offensive security. Website: subverted.io (academy.subverted.io). — same source
- Traction: launched Proactive at the ITWeb Security Summit 2026 in Johannesburg (June 2026). No funding disclosed. — same source

**C Star / C STAR Holdings (Pty) Ltd (Deeptech)**
- Spin-off from the Catalysis Institute in UCT's Department of Chemical Engineering, Cape Town. It is developing technology to produce sustainable hydrocarbon (liquid) fuels from CO2, and enables decentralised fuel production. — [Engineering News](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/uct-spin-off-c-star-selected-for-breakthrough-energy-fellows-programme-2025-09-19); [UCT News](https://www.news.uct.ac.za/article/-2025-10-24-uct-spinout-selected-into-global-fellowship-to-drive-clean-fuel-innovation)
- Problem: decarbonising liquid fuels by recycling CO2 into drop-in fuels. Likely customers are industrial emitters and fuel users (inferred, see Inferences).
- Founded 2022 to commercialise about three decades of UCT research. Co-founders/directors include Prof Nico Fischer and Prof Michael Claeys. COO: Dr Wijnand Marquart. — [UCT RCI](https://uct.ac.za/rci/articles/2025-09-19-uct-spin-company-selected-breakthrough-energy-fellow); [UCT EBE](https://ebe.uct.ac.za/node/578831)
- Recognition: selected for the 2025 Breakthrough Energy Fellows cohort, the first Africa-based fellows team. — [Engineering News](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/uct-spin-off-c-star-selected-for-breakthrough-energy-fellows-programme-2025-09-19)
- Funding: was raising a pre-seed round in late 2025, with interest from UCT's Evergreen Fund and international VCs. — [UCT News](https://www.news.uct.ac.za/article/-2025-10-24-uct-spinout-selected-into-global-fellowship-to-drive-clean-fuel-innovation)

**Nanosene (Pty) Ltd (Deeptech)**
- Stellenbosch University spin-out, founded 2022. It designs and supplies bespoke amphiphilic polymers, branded BzAM, that extract membrane proteins (key drug targets) from cell membranes without harsh detergents. Its customers are pharma, biotech and academic structural-biology labs. Bills itself as Africa's first bespoke polymer supplier. — [SU Innovus](https://www.su.ac.za/en/innovus/technology-transfer/nanosene-pty-ltd); [SU Research for Impact](https://www0.sun.ac.za/researchforimpact/2024/09/26/bespoke-polymers-put-su-start-up-on-map); [KRISP](https://krisp.org.za/news.php?id=966)
- Problem: membrane proteins are hard to isolate intact, which slows drug-target identification and drug discovery. — [KRISP "Unlocking the Hardest Proteins in Biology"](https://krisp.org.za/news.php?id=966)
- Founders: Dr Gestél Kuyler (CEO, polymer scientist) and Prof Bert Klumperman. COO: Dr Elaine Barnard. City: Stellenbosch. — [KRISP](https://krisp.org.za/news.php?id=966); [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/polymer-innovator-helps-to-transform-world-of-medicine-673131a)
- Traction:
  - manufacturing and distribution partnership with Cube Biotech (Germany)
  - polymers being evaluated by partners in the UK and US
  - selected for Swiss Residency Week and the SA delegation at VivaTech 2024
  - pitched at SWEAT Africa 2026 in the Open Startup BRAIN 5.0 cohort
  - plans to expand into drug delivery, microscopy and screening assays
  — [SU Innovus](https://www.su.ac.za/en/innovus/technology-transfer/nanosene-pty-ltd); [KRISP SWEAT Africa](https://krisp.org.za/news.php?id=1003)
- No funding amounts found. The Dealroom profile has blank investment fields. — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/nanosene)

**Safeguard Technologies / SafeGuard Technologies (Deeptech)**
- Best candidate: a student-led Stellenbosch startup building a multi-layered personal-safety system. The system combines mobile apps, a wearable panic button that works without a phone or mobile network, and drone response. It is in its second development cycle, integrating a drone with the panic button. — [Die Matie](https://diematie.com/2025/09/student-led-startup-secures-funding-for-crime-fighting-drones/)
- Problem: personal safety and slow emergency response in South Africa, including where there is no phone or network coverage. — same source
- Founder: Tumelo Mokoena, a second-year Electrical Engineering student at Stellenbosch University (2025). — same source
- Traction: placed second in the 2025 RISP Regional Innovation Challenge and won R100,000 in development support. — same source
- Only one source covers this company (the SU student newspaper). Unrelated similarly named firms include SafeGuard Cyber (US) and SGX SecureGuard (Pretoria). — [Capterra SA](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/207237/safeguard-cyber); [MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/84574)

### Inferences
- The five identified finalists are all plausibly eligible (South African, under 5 years old, pre-Series A):
  - C Star and Nanosene were founded in 2022.
  - SafeGuard is a 2025 student venture.
  - Community Wolf raised only seed-sized sums (about R8.3m plus Baobab).
  - Subverted discloses no funding.
- Community Wolf sits in AI & Big Data because of its "community intelligence"/data-insights layer. Subverted sits there because it positions itself around "the human-AI era". Neither is a pure AI model company.
- C Star's likely customers (industrial CO2 emitters, fuel producers, off-grid fuel users) are inferred from "decentralised production of sustainable hydrocarbon fuels from CO2". No source names customers.
- SafeGuard Technologies is the likely Deeptech finalist because it combines hardware, drones and a network-independent wearable, which fits a deeptech category. It is not confirmed against the official list.
- The four unidentified names (Brand Scan, KRITIQ, Kwanda AI, Tavas) are probably very early or stealth-stage, with little press. Their own websites or LinkedIn pages, or the official finalist list on startupclubza.com, would be needed.
