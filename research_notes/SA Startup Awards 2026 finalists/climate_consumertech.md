# SA Startup Awards 2026 finalists: Climate & Sustainability and Consumertech

Scope: profiles of 8 finalists (Climate & Sustainability: MeYouUs, Switch Energy (Pty) Ltd, WiggleWoo Worm Farm; Consumertech: Bites-pricecompare, Doisk, Driveway Tyres, VezoPay, VISTO). Research date: 18 Nov 2026.

Method note: the official awards page (startupclubza.com/awards) is a JavaScript single-page app. Its static HTML holds no finalist data, so I could not read finalist blurbs, websites or founder names from it. I did not confirm any company's identity against the official listing. I matched each one by name, SA location and fit with its category.

Award eligibility, for checking each match: the startup must be a tech venture less than five years old, pre-Series A, with a majority of its presence in South Africa. It needs an active product in market and must be post-revenue or showing substantial user growth. Entries closed 30 Oct 2026, public voting ran 2-6 Nov 2026, and winners are announced 18 Nov 2026 in Cape Town — [Disrupt Africa](https://disruptafrica.com/2026/10/26/entries-open-for-2026-south-african-startup-awards/)

## What exactly does each product do, and for whom?

### Takeaway
Climate: MeYouUs is a conservation-donation platform where donors sponsor mapped squares of protected land. Switch Energy is B2B smart-metering, utility-data and energy-wheeling software. WiggleWoo is a weekly food-waste collection and worm-composting subscription in Cape Town's Deep South.

Consumertech: Bites is a free app for comparing restaurant menu prices in Cape Town. Doisk is a small AI and app studio whose consumer products are Enfant (an AI baby-cry assistant) and Stylit (a beauty booking marketplace). Driveway Tyres sells tyres online and fits them at your home or work in Gauteng. VezoPay makes tap-to-pay smart rings that link to bank cards. VISTO is a smart-TV "digital concierge" app for Airbnb, guesthouse and hotel hosts.

**WiggleWoo Worm Farm (Climate & Sustainability)**
- Website: https://wigglewoo.co.za/. It is a Cape Town food-waste collection and vermicast business — [WiggleWoo](https://wigglewoo.co.za/)
- Service: households get a 10L bucket for food scraps, which is swapped weekly. Earthworms turn the scraps into vermicast and worm tea, and each household gets 2L of vermicast back each month. - Not the same as the WiggleWoo phonics app for children (wigglewoo.app) — [wigglewoo.app](https://wigglewoo.app/)

## What pain point does each address (SA context)?

### Takeaway
Most of these products target everyday South African pain points: the cost of living (Bites), unreliable and fragmented utility billing in a market of rising tariffs and growing private power trading (Switch), food waste going to landfill (WiggleWoo), the hassle of tyre shops (Driveway), and payment friction and phone-theft risk (VezoPay). MeYouUs, Doisk's Enfant and VISTO address more general problems.

### Cited Findings
- **MeYouUs:** people care about conservation but lack easy, tangible ways to take part. In its own words, "The challenge is not willingness — the challenge is infrastructure." It aims to channel funds to protected-area managers — [MeYouUs](https://meyouus.org/)
- **Switch Energy:** inaccurate or fragmented utility data. It turns readings, tariffs and network statements into reliable numbers for billing, finance and energy trading, including the settlement of wheeled energy — [Switch](https://www.switch.org.za/). Context: SA electricity prices are set to rise by about 26% over three years, which is driving energy-startup activity, and Switch supports metering and revenue collection for backyard dwellings in low-income areas — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/03/13/rising-electricity-costs-south-africa-energy-startups/)
- **WiggleWoo:** food waste in landfill produces methane, which the site calls "80x more potent" than CO2. Weekly collection diverts that waste and returns soil-enriching vermicast to households — [WiggleWoo](https://wigglewoo.co.za/)
- **Bites:** diners cannot tell whether a meal is worth its price. Steiner found the same meal at R130 in one restaurant and R79 across the street — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-10-21-r130-or-r79-uct-students-app-helps-diners-find-the-cheaper-bite/)
- **Doisk / Enfant:** helps parents interpret why a baby is crying. Stylit simplifies booking beauty and wellness appointments and pays practitioners fast — [Doisk](https://doisk.com/)
- **Driveway Tyres:** removes the need to visit a tyre shop and wait in a queue, because fitment comes to the customer's home or workplace — [Driveway Tyres Info](https://drivewaytyres.co.za/info/)
- **VezoPay:** pay without carrying a wallet or phone, with no charging or unlocking needed. Tokenisation means a lost ring does not expose card data — [TechCentral](https://techcentral.co.za/vezopay-payment-ring-built-south-africans/247144/). Its main growth constraint is bank onboarding, not technology — [WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2026/08/14/vezopay-payment-ring-bank-bottleneck/)
- **VISTO:** hosts repeatedly answer the same guest questions about Wi-Fi, house info and local tips. VISTO puts this on the guest TV as a branded, multilingual concierge — [visto.host](https://visto.host/us); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.digidash.themillennialcompany&hl=en)

### Inferences
- In SA, carrying a phone or wallet carries a theft risk, which may strengthen VezoPay's appeal. No source states this explicitly.
- For Switch, "wheeling" matters because SA is opening up private electricity trading over municipal and Eskom networks. The site's positioning implies this, but I found no figures on it.

### Gaps
- No independent customer or impact figures for MeYouUs, WiggleWoo, Switch, Driveway Tyres or VISTO.

## Is each the right company (SA, under 5 years, pre-Series A)?

### Takeaway
High-confidence matches: VezoPay, Bites, WiggleWoo, Driveway Tyres and Switch Energy (its "(Pty) Ltd" legal name matches). MeYouUs and Doisk are medium-high. VISTO is medium.

The main eligibility flag is Switch Energy. Its own site says it was founded in 2018, which is about 8 years and outside the "under 5 years" rule, unless the entering entity or product line is newer.

### Cited Findings
- **VezoPay — high.** It is a uniquely named SA smart-ring company with extensive SA press coverage. The first rings launched in July 2024, investors are angels, and no priced institutional round has been reported — [TechCentral](https://techcentral.co.za/vezopay-payment-ring-built-south-africans/247144/); [WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2026/08/14/vezopay-payment-ring-bank-bottleneck/). Its pre-Series A status is not stated explicitly.
- **Bites-pricecompare — high.** A UCT student's Cape Town price-comparison app covered in Oct 2026, with "pricecompare" matching the product — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-10-21-r130-or-r79-uct-students-app-helps-diners-find-the-cheaper-bite/)
- **WiggleWoo Worm Farm — high.** The exact name is used on a Cape Town .co.za site, and the business is clearly early-stage and owner-operated — [WiggleWoo](https://wigglewoo.co.za/); [Capri Watch](https://www.capriwatch.co.za/wigglewoo-worm-farm/)
- **Driveway Tyres — high.** Driveway Tyres (Pty) Ltd in Centurion, with an online store and mobile fitment. Its age is unconfirmed — [Driveway Tyres](https://drivewaytyres.co.za/info/)
- **Switch Energy (Pty) Ltd — high on identity, with an eligibility flag.** It is a Cape Town enertech company, and its site states it was founded in 2018 — [Switch About](https://www.switch.org.za/about-us)
- **MeYouUs — medium-high.** The exact name, an SA (Pty) Ltd operator and a conservation fit with the category. The 2026 registration suggests it is very new — [MeYouUs](https://meyouus.org/)
- **Doisk — medium-high.** The exact unusual name, a Cape Town Pty Ltd, and consumer apps — [Doisk](https://doisk.com/); [TheOrg](https://theorg.com/org/doisk)
- **VISTO — medium.** The developer is South African and the app was listed in April 2026, but the name is generic and the product is aimed at hosts — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.digidash.themillennialcompany&hl=en)

### Inferences
- Switch Energy may have entered on a newer product line (for example its wheeling platform), or Startup Club ZA may have applied the age rule loosely. This is speculation.

### Gaps
- No source links any of these companies to Startup Club ZA, because the official finalist page could not be read.
- Pre-Series A status is not explicitly confirmed for any company. For all eight, no institutional round was found.
